## projects

If you're here, it could mean you want to know a bit more about my projects.

I like data: data in representation spaces, art as data, and language as data. Art and Language are easily (or sometimes not-so-easily) quantifiable into Data and can be projected onto Representation Spaces, which at least makes pretty visualizations. I like to think these projects help keep me thinking about data (conceptually), but they just as often exist and appear as problems to solve and tinker with. Transforming existing data shows us something new, and I adore novelty. 

I mostly work in Python when I can, but I've worked with Go, C/C++, Swift, Lua, and basically anything that a Google search tells me is "the best" for a particular use. You can look on my [GitHub](https://github.com/amilleah) for anything I might've missed.

These are just a few projects I'm highlighting, mostly due to their demands for data processing and visualization (or because I like them the most) but I'm often coding small tools or developing programs. Most are open source, some are too put-together-with-duct-tape to provide to others.

Reach out to me if you want to chat about anything that you find!
