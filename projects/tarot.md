## tarot

A daily tarot draw. One card from the 78-card Rider-Waite-Smith deck, rendered as ANSI art from .ans files made with [chafa](https://hpjansson.org/chafa/). Cached to your browser and redrawn each day.

The Go TUI lets you draw and reshuffle the full deck in your terminal. [github.com/amilleah/tarot](https://github.com/amilleah/tarot)
