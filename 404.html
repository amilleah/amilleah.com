Amilleah /əˈmɪliə/ is a cognitive neuroscientist and linguist.

Hi! I'm an incoming Linguistics PhD student at the University of Michigan, where I'll be working with Prof. Jon Brennan. I study how we represent and use context during real-time language processing. I use M/EEG and neural language models to investigate these topics. Previously, I worked with Prof. Liina Pylkkänen at the NYU Neurolinguistics Lab.

I am often developing open-source projects, working with time-series data, writing my newsletter, and updating this website!
